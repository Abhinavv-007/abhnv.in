    Tracking lifetime purchases

    How to track one-time lifetime purchases and permanent licenses in SubTrack

    What are Lifetime Subscriptions?

    Lifetime subscriptions are one-time purchases that give you permanent access to a service without recurring payments. Think of software licenses, lifetime app upgrades, or one-time membership fees.

    How to Add a Lifetime Purchase

    When adding or editing a subscription:

    1. In the Billing Cycle dropdown, select "Lifetime"
    2. Enter the one-time cost you paid
    3. Set the purchase date as the billing date
    4. Save your subscription

    That's it! SubTrack will now track this as a lifetime investment.

    Where Lifetime Purchases Appear

    Lifetime subscriptions are treated differently from recurring subscriptions:

    • Separate Section: They appear in a dedicated "Lifetime Investments" section in your Analytics
    • Cost Calculations: They don't count toward your active monthly subscription costs
    • No Renewals: The billing date never updates since there are no future payments
    • Historical Tracking: Perfect for seeing how much you've invested in permanent tools and services

    Examples of Lifetime Purchases

    • One-time software licenses (Microsoft Office standalone)
    • Lifetime app upgrades (pro versions with one-time payment)
    • Permanent memberships or access passes
    • Educational courses with lifetime access
    • Lifetime cloud storage plans
