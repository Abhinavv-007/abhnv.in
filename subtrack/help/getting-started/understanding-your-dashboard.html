Understanding your dashboard

Overview of the main features and sections in your SubTrack dashboard

Dashboard Overview

Your SubTrack dashboard gives you a complete overview of all your subscriptions at a glance.

Main Dashboard

When you first sign in, you'll see:

  • Total monthly cost - All subscriptions converted to monthly equivalent for easy comparison
  • Active subscriptions count - How many subscriptions you're currently tracking
  • Upcoming renewals - Subscriptions renewing soon (next 7-30 days)
  • Quick actions - Add subscription, view all, check notifications

Subscriptions Page

This is where you manage all your subscriptions:

  • List view - See all subscriptions with cost, next billing date, and status
  • Sort options - Sort by price, name, or renewal date
  • Filter by category - View subscriptions by Entertainment, Productivity, etc.
  • Quick edit - Click any subscription to edit details or mark as cancelled

Analytics & Insights

Get deeper insights into your spending:

  • Cost breakdown - View spending by category, family member, or billing cycle
  • Spending trends - See how your subscription costs change over time

Categories

Organize subscriptions your way:

  • Create custom categories (Entertainment, Productivity, Health, etc.)
  • Assign colors to each category for easy visual sorting
  • View total spending per category

Family Members

Track who uses which subscriptions:

  • Add yourself and family members
  • Set a default member for quick subscription entry
  • Assign subscriptions to specific family members

Notifications

Never miss a renewal:

  • Email notifications - Get reminded via email
  • Push notifications - Receive alerts on your device (PWA only)
  • In-app notifications - Check your notification center anytime
  • Mark all as read - Clear notifications with one click
