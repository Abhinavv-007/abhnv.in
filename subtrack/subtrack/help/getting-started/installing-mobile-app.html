    Installing the mobile app

    Step-by-step guide to install SubTrack as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on iOS and Android

    Install SubTrack on Your Phone

    SubTrack is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it on your phone just like a native app - no App Store or Google Play required!

    iOS Installation (Safari)

    1. Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad
    2. Navigate to subtrack.abhnv.in
    3. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up)
    4. Scroll down and select "Add to Home Screen"
    5. Tap "Add" in the top right corner
    6. The SubTrack app icon will appear on your home screen
    7. Important: Always open SubTrack from the home screen icon, not from Safari

    Android Installation (Chrome)

    1. Open Chrome on your Android device
    2. Navigate to subtrack.abhnv.in
    3. Tap the menu button (three dots in the top right)
    4. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
    5. Confirm the installation
    6. The SubTrack app will appear in your app drawer and home screen

    Why install as a PWA?

    • Faster access - Opens instantly from your home screen
    • Offline support - Access your subscription data anytime
    • Push notifications - Get reminded before subscriptions renew
    • Native-like experience - Feels just like a regular app
    • No app store required - Install directly from the web

    Troubleshooting

    Push notifications not working on iOS?

    If push notifications keep saying "enabling" and spinning:

    1. Uninstall the PWA (long press icon > Remove App)
    2. Force quit Safari completely
    3. Wait a few minutes, then reinstall following the steps above
    4. Check Settings: disable Screen Time/Content Restrictions, Low Power Mode, iCloud Private Relay
    5. Use strong WiFi and clear Safari cache if needed
