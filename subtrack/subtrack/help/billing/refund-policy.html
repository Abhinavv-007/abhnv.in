    Refund policy

    SubTrack offers a simple 30-day money-back guarantee on all purchases

    30-Day Money-Back Guarantee

    We want you to be completely satisfied with SubTrack. That's why we offer a simple, no-questions-asked refund policy.

    Our Promise

    If you're not happy with SubTrack for any reason, you can request a full refund within 30 days of your purchase. We won't ask you why - we'll simply process your refund.

    How to Request a Refund

    1. Contact us within 30 days of your purchase date
    2. Reach out via LinkedIn DM to @Vu Nguyen or use the in-app feedback form
    3. Include your purchase email address
    4. We'll process your refund promptly - usually within 1-2 business days

    What Happens After a Refund

    • Your account will revert to the free trial (3 subscriptions)
    • You'll lose access to premium features like unlimited tracking
    • All your data will be preserved in case you decide to purchase again

    Need Help Instead?

    Before requesting a refund, consider reaching out for support. Most issues can be resolved quickly, and we're committed to making SubTrack work for you.

    We respond fast - usually within hours, especially for paying customers.
