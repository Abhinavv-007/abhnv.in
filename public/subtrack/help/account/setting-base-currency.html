Setting Your Base Currency

Choose how subscription totals are displayed

Display Totals in Your Currency

SubTrack lets you choose a base currency so you can understand your spending at a glance.

What Is Base Currency?

It’s the currency used to display total costs across your dashboard, even if subscriptions are billed in different currencies.

How to Set Base Currency

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Find Base Currency
  3. Select your preferred currency
  4. Totals update automatically

How Cost Calculations Work

SubTrack converts all subscriptions to a monthly equivalent for fair comparison.

Example:
$33.90 every 2 weeks
→ $881.40 per year
→ $73.45 per month

Individual Subscription Currencies

You can still enter subscriptions in their original currency. Base currency only affects totals and analytics.
