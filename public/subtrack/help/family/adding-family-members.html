    Adding family members

    Learn how to set up family members in SubTrack to track who uses which subscriptions

    Getting Started with Family Members

    Family members help you track who in your household uses which subscriptions. This makes it easy to see individual usage patterns and understand how subscriptions are distributed across your family.

    Important First Step

    Create yourself as a family member first before adding others. This establishes you as the primary account holder and makes it easier to assign your own subscriptions.

    Step-by-Step Guide

    1. Navigate to the Family Members section in your dashboard
    2. Click Add Family Member
    3. Enter your own name first (e.g., "John" or "Me")
    4. Click Save to create your profile
    5. Repeat the process to add other family members (spouse, children, roommates, etc.)

    What Happens Next?

    Once you've added family members, you can:

    • Assign subscriptions to specific people when adding or editing them
    • View usage breakdowns by family member in your analytics
    • Track who's using which services at a glance
    • Set a default family member for faster subscription entry

    Pro tip: Use simple, recognizable names like "Sarah", "Kids", or "Me" to make it easy to identify who's who when assigning subscriptions.
