    Using categories

    How to organize and group your subscriptions with custom categories

    Organize Your Subscriptions

    Categories help you group subscriptions by type, making it easier to understand where your money goes each month.

    Default Categories

    SubTrack provides common categories out of the box:

    • Entertainment (Netflix, Spotify, Disney+)
    • Productivity (Microsoft 365, Adobe Creative Cloud)
    • Utilities (Phone bill, Internet, Cloud storage)
    • Health & Fitness (Gym membership, Meditation apps)
    • Education (Online courses, Learning platforms)

    Creating Custom Categories

    Want to track subscriptions your own way? Create custom categories:

    1. Go to the Categories page from your dashboard
    2. Click "Add Category"
    3. Choose a name (e.g., "Business Tools", "Gaming", "Family")
    4. Pick a color to visually distinguish the category
    5. Save your new category

    Assigning Categories to Subscriptions

    When adding or editing a subscription:

    • Look for the Category dropdown
    • Select the appropriate category
    • If no category is selected, the subscription will appear as "Uncategorized"

    Category Insights

    Categories unlock powerful insights:

    • See spending breakdowns by category
    • Identify which areas consume the most of your budget
    • Quickly filter and view subscriptions by category
    • Make informed decisions about where to cut costs
