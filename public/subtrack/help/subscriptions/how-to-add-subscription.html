    How to add a subscription

    Step-by-step guide to adding and tracking your subscriptions in SubTrack

    Adding Subscriptions

    Adding a subscription to SubTrack is quick and easy. Here's how:

    Step 1: Navigate to Subscriptions

    Go to the Subscriptions page from your dashboard.

    Step 2: Click "Add Subscription"

    Look for the add button to create a new subscription entry.

    Step 3: Fill in Basic Information

    Enter the following details:

    • Service Name: The name of your subscription (e.g., "Netflix", "Spotify")
    • Cost: The amount you pay per billing cycle
    • Currency: Your subscription's currency
    • Next Billing Date: When your next payment is due

    Step 4: Select Billing Cycle

    Choose how often you're charged:

    • Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, Yearly, or Lifetime
    • For custom cycles (like every 4 weeks), select the base cycle and adjust the interval

    Step 5: Add Optional Details

    You can also add:

    • Category: Organize subscriptions by type (Entertainment, Productivity, etc.)
    • Family Member: Assign to a specific household member
    • Custom Icon: Upload a logo or choose a color
    • Notes: Add any additional details

    Click Save and your subscription is now being tracked!
