    Purchase not syncing to account

    What to do if you've purchased SubTrack but your account still shows the free trial limit

    Purchased But Still Seeing Free Trial?

    If you've paid for SubTrack but your account still shows the 3-subscription limit, don't worry - this is usually an easy fix.

    Most Common Cause: Email Mismatch

    Your purchase email doesn't match your login email. This happens frequently when using different payment methods.

    Common scenarios:

    • Logged in with Google (your Gmail)
    • Paid with Apple Pay (your Apple ID email)
    • The two email addresses are different

    How to Fix It

    Step 1: Check which email you used

    • Check your payment receipt/confirmation email
    • Check which email you're logged in with (visible in Settings)

    Step 2: Contact support

    • Send a LinkedIn DM to @Vu Nguyen
    • Include both email addresses (login + purchase)
    • We'll manually link your purchase to your account

    Step 3: Get activated

    • We usually respond within hours (often faster for paying customers)
    • Your account will be upgraded immediately
    • Start tracking unlimited subscriptions

    Alternative: Log Out and Back In

    If the emails do match, try this:

    1. Log out completely from SubTrack
    2. Close all browser tabs
    3. Clear your browser cache (optional)
    4. Log back in with the same email used for purchase

    We've Got You Covered

    Your purchase is safe - we can see it in our system and will make sure you get what you paid for.
