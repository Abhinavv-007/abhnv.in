Understanding pricing plans

Learn about SubTrack's simple pricing options - free trial, monthly plan, and yearly plan

SubTrack Pricing Plans

SubTrack offers simple, one-time pricing with no auto-renewal or recurring charges.

Free Trial

  • Track up to 3 subscriptions at no cost
  • Full access to all features
  • No credit card required
  • Perfect for testing SubTrack before purchasing

Monthly Plan ($2/mo)

  • Track unlimited subscriptions for one month
  • One-time payment of $2 - no auto-renewal
  • Access to all premium features including family member tracking, notifications, and analytics
  • License expires after one month (you'll need to purchase again to continue)

Yearly Plan ($20/yr)

  • Track unlimited subscriptions for one year
  • One-time payment of $20 - pay once, use for a full year
  • All current and future features included
  • No recurring fees or surprise charges

Important Notes

No Auto-Renewal: Unlike most subscription services, SubTrack licenses are one-time purchases that never auto-renew. You'll never be charged again unless you choose to purchase a new license.

Early Bird Pricing: Special pricing may be available during promotional periods. Prices are subject to change, but your purchase is locked in at the price you pay.

All purchases are protected by our 30-day money-back guarantee.
