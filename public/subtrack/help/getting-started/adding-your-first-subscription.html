Adding your first subscription

Quick guide to adding your first subscription - takes about 10 seconds

Quick Start Guide

Adding a subscription to SubTrack takes less than 10 seconds. Here's how:

Tracking Only

Adding a subscription here simply tracks it. It does not subscribe you to the service or change your billing with the provider.

Quick steps

  1. Navigate to Dashboard > Subscriptions
  2. Click the "Add Subscription" button (the big plus icon)
  3. Fill in the basic information:
    • Service name (e.g., "Netflix", "Spotify")
    • Cost (the amount you pay)
    • Billing cycle (Monthly, Yearly, Weekly, or Lifetime)
    • Next billing date (when it renews next)
  4. Click "Add" and you're done!

Optional details

Want to track more? You can also add:

  • Category (Entertainment, Productivity, etc.)
  • Family member (who uses this subscription)
  • Payment method
  • Custom logo or icon
  • Notes for additional information

Special billing cycles

Need to track subscriptions with unusual billing?

  • Every 4 weeks (like New York Times): Select "Weekly" and set interval to 4
  • Every 2 years (biennial): Select "Yearly" and set interval to 2
  • Lifetime purchases: Select "Lifetime" - these won't count toward your monthly costs

Auto-renew setting

The "Recurring" toggle determines if SubTrack automatically updates the next billing date:

  • ON (Recurring): Automatically rolls forward based on your billing cycle
  • OFF: Marks as expired when the date passes (useful for cancelled subscriptions)

Tracking Only

SubTrack is a tracking tool only. It does not manage, charge for, or cancel your actual subscriptions. You must still pay providers directly.
