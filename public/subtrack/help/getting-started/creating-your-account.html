Creating your account

Learn how to sign up for SubTrack using Google OAuth or magic link authentication

Getting Started

Preview Notice

Authentication is currently in preview. Full account features are coming soon.

Getting started with SubTrack is quick and easy. We offer two simple ways to create your account:

Sign up with Google

  1. Visit subtrack.abhnv.in
  2. Click the "Sign in with Google" button
  3. Select your Google account
  4. Grant permission when prompted
  5. You're in! Start tracking your subscriptions immediately

Sign up with Magic Link

Prefer not to use Google? No problem:

  1. Visit subtrack.abhnv.in
  2. Enter your email address in the sign-in form
  3. Click "Send magic link"
  4. Check your inbox for an email from SubTrack
  5. Click the link in the email to verify and access your account

Important: Make sure to use the same email for both login and any purchases. If you sign in with Google and pay with Apple Pay, the email addresses might differ. If your purchase doesn't activate automatically, contact support at support@abhnv.in with your email address to link your account.

What happens next?

After signing in, you'll go through a quick onboarding flow where you can:

  • Set up your profile
  • Choose your base currency
  • Import existing subscriptions (optional)
  • Add your first subscription manually

That's it! You're ready to take control of your subscriptions.
